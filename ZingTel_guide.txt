ZingTel Complete Service Guide 2024

Chapter 1: Getting Started
To activate your ZingTel SIM, visit any franchise with your original CNIC.
New customers get 1GB free data for the first 3 days.
SIM activation takes up to 2 hours after registration.

Chapter 2: Billing and Payments
Bills are generated on the 1st of every month.
Payment can be made via EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or bank transfer.
Late payment charges of Rs. 50 apply after 15 days.
Account suspension occurs after 30 days of non-payment.

Chapter 3: Technical Support
Technical support is available 24/7 on 111.
Live chat support is available on ZingTel app from 9am to 9pm.
Home visits for technical issues are free for Premium customers.
Standard and Basic customers are charged Rs. 200 for home visits.

Chapter 4: Complaints and Escalation
Complaints are resolved within 3 working days.
Unresolved complaints can be escalated to PTA at pta.gov.pk.
Compensation for service failure is credited within 5 working days.
